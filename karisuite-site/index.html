Who we are
"We build things we wish existed — then we make them available to everyone who needs them."

Kari Suite is a two-person independent software company. We build focused, practical tools — each one starting with a real frustration, a specific moment where the existing options were too expensive, too complicated, or just completely wrong for the job.

We're not a startup chasing a series A. We're not building features to impress investors. We build things we need, make them work properly, and put them in front of the people who need them too.

Right now we're focused on hospitality — one industry, one live product, one real problem solved. Where we go next depends on where the next real problem is. We're not mapping that out in advance.

How we work

The way we build things.

Two people, no process theatre. Just a clear way of deciding what to build and how to build it.

1

Start with a real problem

Every product begins with a specific, felt frustration — something one of us or someone we know has actually experienced. Not a gap in the market. A genuine pain point.

2

Build tight. Ship it.

We scope it small and make it work. No feature lists to impress investors, no roadmap that grows indefinitely. One problem, solved properly, shipped to real users.

3

Works in the real world

Software that only works in ideal conditions isn't finished. We test against the messy reality of how people actually work — then we ship it.
