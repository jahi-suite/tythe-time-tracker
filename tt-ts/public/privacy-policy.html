01

About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Kari Time handles personal data when hospitality venues use the service to record shifts, timesheets, and payroll-related information.

This is a practical draft for a small SaaS business and is not legal advice. Venues using Kari Time should review their own employment and data protection obligations and take independent legal advice where needed.

02

What We Collect

Depending on how a venue uses Kari Time, we may process the following types of data:

  • Staff names and basic staff identifiers
  • Staff roles or job positions
  • Clock-in and clock-out times, shift dates, and worked hours
  • Pay rates and payroll-related shift values entered by managers
  • Venue information such as venue name and team setup details
  • Account and access information — login details, user roles, and basic system logs — so the service can operate securely
  • Website analytics — we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site (e.g. pages visited). This may include IP address, device type, and similar technical data. See our Third Parties section for more detail.
03

Why We Collect and Use Data

We use personal data in Kari Time only to provide the service. This includes:

  • Recording and displaying shifts and timesheets
  • Supporting payroll preparation and exports
  • Maintaining records for compliance and internal checks
  • Providing an audit trail of time entries and manager changes
  • Securing, maintaining, and troubleshooting the platform
For venue staff data, the venue is usually the data controller and Kari Time acts as a service provider (processor) on the venue's behalf.
04

Who Can Access the Data

Access is limited to people who need it to use or support the service:

Venue managers
Can access venue and staff records for their own venue only
Staff
Can access their own records where the product provides staff access
Kari Time
May access data only when reasonably necessary for support, maintenance, security, backups, or troubleshooting
We do not sell personal data. We only access data in line with providing and securing the service.
05

How Long We Keep Data

We keep data for as long as needed to provide Kari Time and for legitimate business and legal reasons, including payroll recordkeeping, audit trail requirements, and dispute resolution.

In practice, records are typically kept until the venue deletes them or closes the account, unless longer retention is required by law or reasonably needed for compliance, security, or backup recovery.

06

Account Closure, Retention & Deletion

If an account is closed, access to the service will be removed. The venue should export any records it needs before closure.

After closure, we will delete or anonymise account data within a reasonable period, except where we need to retain limited information for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security logs, or backup restoration cycles.

07

Third Parties & Hosting

Kari Time may use technical providers — such as hosting and infrastructure services — to run the platform securely. Those providers process data only to the extent needed to host and support the service.

Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics to measure website traffic and how visitors use our site. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect information such as IP address, pages visited, device type, and browser. This helps us improve the site. Google's use of this data is governed by its Privacy Policy. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

We do not share venue staff data with third parties for marketing purposes. If no subprocessor is used for a specific function, data remains stored securely within Kari Time systems.

08

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

No system is completely risk-free, but we work to keep Kari Time secure and limit access appropriately.
09

Your Rights (UK GDPR)

Depending on the circumstances, individuals may have the following rights:

  • Request access to their personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of data (where applicable)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Make a complaint to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)
If your data is held by Kari Time on behalf of a venue, please contact that venue first. We may direct requests to the relevant venue where it acts as data controller.
10

Contact & Complaints

If you have privacy questions or want to request access, correction, or deletion, contact the venue that uses Kari Time or reach Kari Time support using the contact details provided in the service.

Privacy queries

Contact the venue directly, or use the support link in the service

UK Regulator

You may also complain to the ICO if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully — ico.org.uk
